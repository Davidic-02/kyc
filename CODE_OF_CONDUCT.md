# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Focus discussion on the code and outcomes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Abusive or hostile language
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation for conduct violations.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers.
